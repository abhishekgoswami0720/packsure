## Full description  *(max 4000 characters)*

```
OpenLabel — know what's in your food.

OpenLabel turns your phone into a food-label reader. Scan a barcode or the
ingredients label on any packaged product and get a clear, instant read on
what's inside: the ingredients, the additives, and an easy-to-understand
health score.

No account. No ads. No tracking. Works offline.

HOW IT WORKS
• Scan the barcode — OpenLabel looks the product up instantly, on your device
  first, then in the open Open Food Facts database.
• Scan the label — point your camera at the ingredients or nutrition panel and
  OpenLabel reads it right on your phone. This works for any product, even ones
  no database has yet.

WHAT YOU LEARN
• Ingredients in plain language.
• Additives flagged — colours, preservatives, sweeteners and more, with a note
  on why they matter.
• A score out of 10, based on the internationally recognised Nutri-Score method
  (sugar, salt, saturated fat, energy, fibre and protein) — so you can compare
  products at a glance.
• A quick verdict — an at-a-glance sense of whether something is a good everyday
  choice or one to enjoy in moderation.

BUILT FOR INDIA
OpenLabel is designed for the products on Indian shelves and works in English.
Snack, spread, drink or staple — you can check it in seconds.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Your camera scans are processed entirely on your device — photos are never
uploaded. The only thing that ever leaves your phone is the barcode number,
used to look up a product. Your scan history stays on your phone. No analytics,
no ads, no trackers.

WORKS OFFLINE
Thousands of common products are built in, so most scans work with no internet
at all. When you're online, OpenLabel can reach the full Open Food Facts
database of millions of products.

FREE
OpenLabel is free to use.

Scan smarter. Eat with your eyes open.
```
