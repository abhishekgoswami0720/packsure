# OpenLabel — Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 6 July 2026**

OpenLabel ("the app", "we", "us") helps you understand what's in packaged food
by scanning barcodes and ingredient labels. We built OpenLabel to respect your
privacy: it works almost entirely on your device and does not require an
account. This policy explains what information the app does and does not handle.

## The short version

- We do **not** collect personal information.
- We do **not** require an account or login.
- We do **not** use analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind.
- Photos you scan are processed **on your device** and are **never uploaded**.
- The only thing that leaves your device is the **barcode number** you scan,
  sent to Open Food Facts to look up product details.
- Your scan history is stored **only on your device**.

## Camera

OpenLabel uses your device camera so you can scan barcodes and ingredient
labels. When you scan a label, the text is read directly on your device
(on-device OCR). **The images and the extracted text are not sent to us or to
any third party, and are not retained after the scan is processed.** We request
only camera permission; we never access your photo library.

## Barcode lookups (Open Food Facts)

When you scan a barcode that isn't already stored in the app, OpenLabel sends
**only that barcode number** over a secure (HTTPS) connection to **Open Food
Facts** (https://world.openfoodfacts.org) — a free, open database of food
products — to retrieve the product's name, ingredients, and nutrition facts.
This request contains no personal information about you, just the product's
barcode. Open Food Facts' handling of requests is governed by their own privacy
policy: https://world.openfoodfacts.org/privacy

We cache the product details we receive so repeat scans work offline and don't
need to be looked up again.

## Information stored on your device

OpenLabel saves your **scan history** (the products you've scanned and their
results) in a database **on your device only**. This never leaves your phone and
is not backed up to us. You can remove it at any time by clearing your history
in the app or by uninstalling the app.

## Information we do NOT collect

We do not collect or process your name, email, phone number, location, contacts,
device identifiers, advertising ID, or any usage analytics. OpenLabel contains
no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

## Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your information with anyone. The only outbound
data is the barcode lookup described above.

## Children's privacy

OpenLabel is suitable for a general audience and does not knowingly collect any
personal information from anyone, including children.

## Data security

Because OpenLabel keeps your data on your device and transmits only a barcode
number over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection, your exposure is minimal by design.

## Your rights and choices

- **Delete your data:** clear your scan history in the app, or uninstall the app
  to remove all local data.
- **Camera permission:** you can revoke camera access at any time in your device
  settings; the app simply won't be able to scan until it's re-granted.
