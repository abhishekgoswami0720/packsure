clinical_notes Study Types
Human & In-vitro

Citric acid is rapidly metabolized in the human Krebs cycle and generally well-tolerated by gut microbiota.

In-vitro models show mild luminal pH modulation that can support beneficial acid-tolerant species without disrupting overall microbial diversity.

High-concentration intake in sensitive individuals may trigger mild upper-GI irritation or reflux symptoms.

Most microbiome-specific studies evaluate isolated citric acid in-vitro rather than whole-food matrices over long-term human cohorts.
