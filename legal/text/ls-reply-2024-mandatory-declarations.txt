(a) to (f) :  The Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011 requires for declaration of mandatory 
information like name and address of the manufacturer/ packer/ importer, country of origin, common or 
generic name of the commodity, net quantity, month and year of manufacture, MRP, Unit Sale Price, best 
before/ use by date in case of commodity become unfit for human consumption, consumer care details etc. on 
all pre-packaged commodities in the interest of consumers. 
  
           The rules are amended from time to time for ease of doing business and to protect the interest of 
consumers.  The Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011 were amended after complying all 
process i.e. consultation with all stake holders, vetting by Ministry of Law & Justice, etc. vide GSR 722 (E) 
dated 6th October, 2023 for declaration of unit sale price in addition to retail sale price (MRP) and declaration 
of month and year of manufacture.  The rules came into foce w.e.f. 1st January, 2024. 
  
           This will help in establishing uniform requirements for packaged commodities, promoting consistency 
and fairness and will help consumers in making informed choices based on complete information. 
 
           Rule 3 (c) of the Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011 exempt the 
packaged commodities meant for industrial or institutional consumers from provisions applicable to packages 
intended for retail sale.
